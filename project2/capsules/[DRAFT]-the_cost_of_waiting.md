# The Cost of Waiting

*(SAMPLE ARTICLE in `[DRAFT]` state - the Composer has shaped the author's raw text and handed it to the Critic.)*

Content states are bracket **prefixes** and move like this:

```
[DRAFT] -> [REVISION] -> [DRAFT] -> [FINAL]-Capsule_N_Topic -> [COVERED]-Capsule_N_Topic
(Composer)  (Critic)    (Composer)   (Critic: number+place)    (Designer: ticketed)
```

The **Critic** owns placement: it decides the number N, renumbers existing `[COVERED]` articles when inserting mid-sequence, fixes every cross-reference, and updates the staircase map. **Slugs never change** - URLs stay stable while numbers shift.

---

Waiting feels free. It is the most expensive thing you will do today...
